# Demo Guide

Use a dedicated demo database and reversible accounts. Never display production credentials or private records.

1. Login and demonstrate role-based denial using a limited account.
2. Dashboard summary.
3. Students and guardians.
4. Academic years, classes, sections, subjects, and assignments.
5. Attendance marking and reporting.
6. Examination setup, marks, and published results.
7. Finance invoices/payments and payroll periods/payslips.
8. Reports, analytics, safe CSV, saved/custom reports.
9. Users/Roles protections, Audit Log, and Login Activity.
10. Settings, responsive layouts, and dark mode.
11. Explain parameterized SQL, session/role gates, CSV formula protection, self-deactivation protection, and last-Super-Admin protection.
12. Show the schema relationships and deployment verification queries.

Create demo accounts through Users & Roles in the demo environment, assign only the minimum role needed, and delete them with dependent activity/audit rows after the presentation.
